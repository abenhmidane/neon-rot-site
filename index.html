Neon Rot

A turn-based infection puzzle for iPhone and Android.

Support

Stuck, found a bug, or a purchase did not unlock? Write to bhmiden.anouar@gmail.com and say which phone and which iOS or Android version you use.

Common questions

  • I bought the full game and it is locked on my new phone. Open the start menu, tap Sectors, tap any locked sector, then tap Restore. Use the same Apple Account (iPhone) or Google account (Android) you bought it with. A purchase made on iPhone does not carry over to Android, or the other way round: the two stores are separate.
  • I cannot solve a sector. Tap Hint under the board. It shows the next move of the shortest solution, and you can tap it again after every move.
  • How do I turn the music off? Start menu, or the Sectors screen, has a Music switch. The speaker button on the board turns all sound off.
  • Does it need the internet? No. Only buying or restoring the unlock needs a connection.
  • Where is my progress kept? On your phone only. Deleting the app deletes it. On Android, your phone's own backup may bring it back on a new phone.
