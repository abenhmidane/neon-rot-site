# Neon Rot: privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026.

Neon Rot does not collect, store or share any personal information.

- **No accounts.** The game has no sign-in and never asks who you are.
- **No tracking, analytics or advertising.** The app contains no analytics, advertising or
  other third-party services, and it makes no network requests of its own.
- **Your progress stays on your device.** Completed sectors, best scores and your sound
  settings are saved in the app's own storage on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **Purchases are handled by Apple.** The one in-app purchase is processed by the App Store
  under Apple's privacy policy. The app only learns whether the purchase exists for your
  Apple Account, so that it can unlock the sectors and restore them on another device.
  It never sees your name, email address or payment details.
- **Children.** Because nothing is collected, nothing is collected from children either.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new
effective date.
