Neon Rot

Privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026. This policy covers Neon Rot on iPhone and on Android.

Neon Rot does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • No accounts. The game has no sign-in and never asks who you are.
  • No tracking, analytics or advertising. The game contains no analytics and no advertising, and the game itself makes no network requests. The only outside service in it is the store's own purchase system, described below.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Completed sectors, best scores and your sound settings are saved in the app's own storage on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them. On Android, if you have turned on your phone's backup, Android may keep a copy in your own Google account so it can return on a new phone; the developer never receives it.
  • Purchases are handled by the store. The one in-app purchase is processed by the App Store under Apple's privacy policy on iPhone, and by Google Play under Google's privacy policy on Android. On Android the app includes Google's Play Billing library for this, which communicates with Google Play. The app only learns whether the purchase exists for your Apple Account or Google account, so that it can unlock the sectors and restore them on another device. It never sees your name, email address or payment details.
  • Children. Because nothing is collected, nothing is collected from children either.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
